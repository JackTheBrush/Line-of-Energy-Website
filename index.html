FILE 01

Manifest of Record

Line of Energy started in September 2020 as a handful of drivers who wanted convoys that ran on schedule and a community worth logging back into. We've since grown into a full virtual trucking company with a regular events calendar across Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator.

We're a proud member of Brave Alliance and run joint convoys with partner VTCs including United Convoys — more events on the calendar, more drivers to run with.

No DLC requirements. No minimum account age. Just drive safe and show up.

FILE 02

The Rulebook

01

Drive Clean

Follow TruckersMP traffic rules at all times, in convoy and outside it. Reports reflect on the whole VTC.

02

Show Up Or Speak Up

RSVP to events you plan to attend. Can't make it? A heads-up in Discord goes a long way.

03

Represent The Tag

Keep in-game names and liveries in line with VTC branding during official convoys.

04

Respect The Convoy

No griefing, no blocking, no reckless overtakes. Convoy Marshals' calls are final on event days.

05

Keep It Civil

Discord and in-game chat are for everyone — harassment or discrimination means immediate removal.

06

Stay Active

Extended inactivity without notice may move you to inactive roster — just message leadership first.
